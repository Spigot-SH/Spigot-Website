# Modal

> Components · Shipped · Spigot design system
> `/designsystem#/modal`

Destructive actions sit far left, separated from the safe pair. This app has irreversible operations and they must not sit adjacent to Cancel.

_Rendered example._

## When to use

- A decision that has to block the page. Routine creation belongs on its own route, not behind an overlay.

## Behaviour

- Focus defaults to `Cancel` on anything destructive.
- Escape and outside-click dismiss, except on the destructive confirmation, where the only ways out are the two buttons.
- Focus returns to the trigger on close, so a keyboard user does not restart at the top of the page.

## Content

- The title is a Title Case statement, never a question.
- The primary button repeats the title's verb. `Cancel` stays literally Cancel.
- An irreversible action ends with *This cannot be undone.*

## Accessibility

- `aria-labelledby` points at the title.
- After a failure, focus stays inside the modal so the error is reachable; after success it returns to the trigger.
