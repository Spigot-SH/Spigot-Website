# Pagination

> Components · Shipped · Spigot design system
> `/designsystem#/pagination`

Numbered pages with a truncating middle. Always paired with a count — "page 3" means nothing without knowing there are nine.

## Default

_Rendered example._

```
Current page: bg-panel + border, neutral. Disabled: 35% opacity, cursor default, and
              genuinely disabled — not just styled to look it. Always render the count alongside.
              "Showing 1-10 of 87" is the information; the numbers are only the control.
```

## When to use

- Moving between pages of the same set. Revealing more of one list is a show-more, not pagination.

## Behaviour

- At the first or last page the slot is empty rather than disabled. An empty rail reads cleaner than a dead link with nowhere to go.

## Content

- The label names the destination. `Previous`, `Next` and the chevron are drawn by the component. Do not write them into the label.

## Accessibility

- Each control announces its direction and its destination, not just *next*.
